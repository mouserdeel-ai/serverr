Building Insulation

Energy-efficient polyurethane insulation for roofs, walls, and building envelopes

Polyurethane: The Gold Standard in Building Insulation

Rigid polyurethane foam is one of the most effective insulation materials available for buildings. With thermal conductivity as low as 0.018 W/m·K, PU foam significantly outperforms traditional insulation materials, reducing energy consumption and heating/cooling costs.

Our polyurethane systems are used in sandwich panels, spray foam, and injection molding applications for residential, commercial, and industrial buildings worldwide.

Roof Insulation

Spray foam and panel systems for flat and sloped roofs. Provides seamless air barrier and superior thermal performance.

Wall Insulation

PU panels and cores for composite walls. Excellent moisture resistance and long-term dimensional stability.

HVAC Ducts

Prefabricated PU panels for air duct systems. Lightweight, easy to install, and minimal thermal bridging.

Recommended Materials

Rigid PU Foam Board

High-density rigid foam boards with excellent thermal performance and structural strength. Available in various thicknesses and sizes.

  • Thermal conductivity: 0.022-0.028 W/m·K
  • Density: 30-60 kg/m³
  • Compressive strength: >150 kPa
  • Fire retardant grades available

Spray Foam System

Two-component spray foam system for seamless insulation application. Expands to fill gaps and voids completely.

  • Closed-cell structure
  • Excellent adhesion
  • Fast expansion and cure
  • Zero VOC formulations
