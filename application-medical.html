Polyurethane for Healthcare

Medical-grade polyurethane materials are essential for patient care equipment, offering superior comfort, pressure redistribution, and durability. Our PU systems meet strict quality standards for healthcare applications.

Used in wheelchairs, hospital beds, orthotic devices, and therapy equipment. Our memory foam and flexible PU materials provide excellent pressure relief and patient comfort.

Wheelchair Cushions

Pressure-relieving PU foam cushions preventing pressure ulcers. Multiple densities for targeted support.

Orthopedic Insoles

Custom-molded PU insoles for diabetic shoes and arch support. Provides cushioning and pressure redistribution.

Hospital Bedding

Memory foam mattresses and overlays for patient comfort. Reduces pressure points and improves sleep quality.
