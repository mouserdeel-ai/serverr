Rigid Foam

Rigid Polyurethane Foam Systems

Our rigid PU foam provides exceptional thermal insulation with closed-cell structure. It offers outstanding dimensional stability, high compressive strength, and excellent moisture resistance. Available in various densities to meet specific application requirements.

Key Specifications

Density: 28-120 kg/m³
Thermal Conductivity: 0.018-0.025 W/m·K
Compressive Strength: 150-500 kPa
Closed Cell Content: >90%

Applications

Building Insulation Cold Storage Pipe Insulation Refrigerated Transport Sandwich Panels
Flexible Foam

Flexible Polyurethane Foam Systems

Our flexible PU foam offers superior cushioning, high resilience, and excellent energy absorption. The open-cell structure provides breathability and comfort. Custom formulations available for specific hardness levels and processing methods.

Key Specifications

Density: 15-80 kg/m³
Hardness: 15-80 Shore A
Resilience: 30-70%
Tensile Strength: 80-300 kPa

Applications

Footwear Insoles Seat Cushions Helmet Padding Protective Equipment Medical Orthotics
Specialty

Integral Skin Polyurethane Foam

Self-skinning PU foam with dense outer skin and cellular core. Provides smooth, durable surfaces without additional finishing. Ideal for armrests, handles, and components requiring both comfort and structural integrity.

Key Specifications

Skin Thickness: 2-8 mm
Core Density: 100-400 kg/m³
Surface Hardness: 60-90 Shore A
Skin Density: 600-1200 kg/m³

Applications

Armrests Handles Steering Wheels Furniture Trim Automotive Interiors
Specialty

Viscoelastic Memory Foam

Slow-recovery PU foam that conforms to body shape under pressure and heat. Provides excellent pressure distribution and comfort. Ideal for medical, seating, and premium consumer products.

Key Specifications

Density: 40-100 kg/m³
Recovery Time: 3-10 seconds
ILD: 8-25
Temperature Sensitive

Applications

Mattress Toppers Wheelchair Cushions Medical Padding Ear Cushions Sport Equipment
