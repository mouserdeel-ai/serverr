Industrial Applications

High-performance polyurethane for industrial sealing, insulation, and custom components

Industrial-Grade Polyurethane

Polyurethane is widely used in industrial applications due to its excellent mechanical properties, chemical resistance, and durability. Our industrial PU systems serve demanding environments in petrochemical, manufacturing, and infrastructure sectors.

We offer rigid foam for pipe insulation, elastomers for seals and gaskets, and custom formulations for specific industrial requirements including fire resistance and high-temperature performance.

Pipe Insulation

Rigid PU foam for oil, gas, and chemical pipelines. Prevents heat loss and condensation in industrial systems.

Seals & Gaskets

PU elastomers for dynamic and static seals. Excellent wear resistance and chemical compatibility.

Custom Moldings

Tailored PU components for specific industrial equipment. From concept to production support.
