Footwear Solutions

High-performance polyurethane for insoles, midsoles, and outsoles

Polyurethane: The Ultimate Footwear Material

Polyurethane is the material of choice for premium footwear due to its excellent cushioning, durability, and design flexibility. Our PU systems are used by major shoe manufacturers worldwide for work boots, sports shoes, casual footwear, and orthopaedic shoes.

We offer both rigid and flexible foam systems, along with microcellular elastomers for outsoles. Custom hardness, density, and color formulations available.

Insoles

Flexible PU foam with excellent shock absorption and comfort. Available in multiple densities and thicknesses.

Midsoles

Lightweight PU foam providing superior cushioning and energy return. Dual-density options for enhanced performance.

Outsoles

Durable PU elastomer with excellent abrasion resistance. Microcellular structure for grip and flexibility.

Recommended Materials

Flexible PU Foam System

Open-cell or semi-closed cell foam for insole applications. Provides excellent cushioning and breathability.

  • Density: 15-60 kg/m³
  • Hardness: 15-50 Shore A
  • High resilience
  • Custom colors available

Microcellular PU Elastomer

Closed-cell foam for outsoles and heel pads. Combines flexibility with excellent wear resistance.

  • Density: 400-600 kg/m³
  • Hardness: 40-70 Shore A
  • Excellent abrasion resistance
  • Oil and slip resistant
