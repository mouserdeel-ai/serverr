Our Polyurethane Materials

Two core material types for endless possibilities

Rigid Polyurethane Foam

Excellent thermal insulation with closed-cell structure. Ideal for building insulation, cold chain storage, and industrial pipe insulation.

  • Low thermal conductivity
  • High compressive strength
  • Moisture resistant
  • Fast demolding
Learn More

Flexible Polyurethane Foam

High resilience and comfort with open-cell structure. Perfect for footwear, seating, cushioning, and impact protection applications.

  • Superior cushioning
  • High elasticity
  • Energy absorbing
  • Customizable hardness
