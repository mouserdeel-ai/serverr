About IF Chemical

Professional polyurethane material manufacturer delivering high-performance solutions

Our Story

IF Chemical is a professional manufacturer specializing in polyurethane materials and customized system solutions. Located in Foshan, Guangdong Province, we serve customers worldwide with high-performance PU foam and elastomer systems.

With years of experience in polyurethane chemistry and applications, our team provides expert technical support from product selection to production optimization. We are committed to delivering reliable, eco-friendly polyurethane materials that meet the diverse needs of modern industries.

Our Mission

To provide industries with high-performance, sustainable polyurethane materials that improve energy efficiency, product performance, and user comfort.

We combine advanced chemistry with practical application expertise to develop solutions that address real-world challenges in insulation, cushioning, sealing, and protection across diverse sectors.
