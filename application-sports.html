Sports Protection

Advanced polyurethane impact protection for athletes and outdoor enthusiasts

Impact Protection That Saves Lives

Polyurethane foam is critical for sports safety equipment, absorbing impact energy to protect athletes from injuries. Our PU systems are used in helmets, body armor, protective pads, and cushioning for various sports equipment.

We offer energy-absorbing foams, viscoelastic memory foams, and rigid protective shells. Custom formulations for specific impact requirements and environmental conditions.

Helmet Liners

Multi-density PU foam for superior impact absorption. Used in cycling, skiing, construction, and military helmets.

Body Protection

Flexible PU padding for vests, shorts, and guards. Comfortable fit with excellent impact distribution.

Equipment Cushioning

Handlebar grips, saddle padding, and racquet sports equipment. Combines comfort with durability.

Recommended Materials

Energy Absorbing PU Foam

Closed-cell rigid foam designed to absorb and dissipate impact energy. Ideal for helmet liners and protective pads.

  • High impact energy absorption
  • Multiple density options
  • Lightweight
  • Temperature stable

Viscoelastic Memory Foam

Slow-recovery foam that conforms to body shape. Provides superior comfort and pressure distribution.

  • Pressure relieving
  • Energy absorbing
  • Conforms to geometry
  • Reusable
